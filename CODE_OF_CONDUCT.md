# Code of Conduct

ArtistPack exists to help independent artists get seen and supported.
That purpose sets the bar for how people treat each other here.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of contributors, artists, and users, including people who
  disagree with a technical decision.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming malice or incompetence.
- Give and receive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect that founding and participating artists are consulted on
  creator-facing decisions (attribution, licensing, cropping rules,
  support links) — see `docs/architecture.md` §"Governance". Engineers do
  not get to override artist input on artist-facing product decisions by
  virtue of writing the code.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Using this project or its community to promote scraping, uncredited
  reuse, or exploitation of artists' work — the opposite of what
  ArtistPack exists to prevent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other communication channel maintainers designate as official.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the maintainers listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently remove anyone who violates this code of conduct, proportional
to severity and pattern of behavior.

This document draws on the spirit of the Contributor Covenant without
reproducing its exact text verbatim; a project may adopt the Contributor
Covenant formally in a later revision if the community prefers it.
